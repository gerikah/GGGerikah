03 / Process

How I work

Step 01

Understand the problem

Start with the questions worth asking: who is this for, what are they trying to do, and what gets in the way. Research, flows, and honest constraints before pixels.

Step 02

Shape the interface

Turn the problem into structure — hierarchy, layout, and states — until the screen makes the next step obvious at a glance.

Step 03

Build the experience

Translate the design into a responsive, accessible front end. Real components, real data, real performance — not just a pretty mockup.

Step 04

Refine the details

Spacing, motion, edge cases, empty states. The last ten percent is where the work starts to feel considered.
